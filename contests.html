🎁 Prizes

Prizes are funded by sponsors and supporters and announced at the start of each month — typically astronomy gear (binoculars, eyepieces, star atlases, smart-telescope accessories), gift cards, and a feature on our home page and newsletter. Every finalist receives a digital certificate.

Help grow next month’s prize pool: chip in or become a contest sponsor.

⚖️ Judging

  • 40% — impact & beauty
  • 30% — technique relative to equipment (a great phone shot can beat an average observatory image)
  • 20% — the story/notes you share about the observation
  • 10% — fit with the monthly theme

Judges are experienced amateur astronomers and educators; sponsors never judge.

Submit your entry

Upload your image to any public host you control (Google Drive / Dropbox share link, Flickr, AstroBin, Instagram post) and paste the link. One entry per category per month.

By entering you confirm the work is your own, captured or drawn by you (or your child), and you grant Star.Academy a non-exclusive license to display it with credit. You keep your copyright.

Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase necessary. Entry is free. A purchase or donation does not improve your chances of winning.
  2. Eligibility: open worldwide to individuals 18+; minors aged 8–17 may enter the Young Astronomer category only via a parent or legal guardian. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Employees and judges, and their households, are not eligible.
  3. Entry period: from the 1st day of each calendar month 00:00 UTC to the last day 23:59 UTC.
  4. Originality: entries must be the entrant’s own work, captured or created by them, not AI-generated, and must not infringe anyone’s rights. Processing is allowed; composites must be disclosed.
  5. Judging & winners: by the criteria above; decisions are final. Winners are notified by email and must reply within 14 days or an alternate is selected. Prizes are non-transferable; equivalent substitutes may be offered. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
  6. License: entrants keep copyright and grant Star.Academy a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to display entries on the site, newsletter and social channels with credit.
  7. Privacy: entry data is used only to run the contest and as described in our Privacy Policy.
