About

A free academy for the oldest science

Everyone has looked up and wondered. Star.Academy exists to turn that wonder into the simple, lasting skill of knowing the sky — for free, in any country, with any budget.

What we do

We combine three things that usually live on separate websites: live sky data computed for your exact location, a structured beginner course with real nightly tasks, and honest guidance on gear, events and travel. On top of that we run a monthly contest that celebrates observers using phones and pencils as much as observatories.

Editorial standards

  • Accuracy first. Sky positions, times and events are calculated with the open-source astronomy-engine library, validated against published almanacs. Live data comes from primary public sources (NASA, NOAA SWPC, The Space Devs, RocketLaunch.Live, Spaceflight News API).
  • Beginner-first language. We explain jargon or avoid it.
  • Independent advice. Gear advice is brand-neutral. Sponsored placements are always labelled and never change recommendations.
  • Safety. We never suggest looking at the Sun without certified solar filters.
  • Corrections. Spotted an error? Tell us and we’ll fix it quickly.

How we’re funded

Star.Academy is supported by advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, optional partner introductions when visitors request them, and voluntary donations. That mix lets us keep every lesson and tool free.
