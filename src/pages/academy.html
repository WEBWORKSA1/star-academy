What you’ll learn

Night Sky 101 follows the same progression used by astronomy clubs and observing programs: first train your eyes, then learn the Moon and a handful of anchor constellations, then use those anchors to find planets, star colors, clusters, nebulae and galaxies. Along the way you’ll learn how to choose binoculars and telescopes without wasting money, how to spot the International Space Station, how meteor showers work and how to photograph the sky with the phone you already own.

Who is it for?

Complete beginners, parents learning with kids, teachers looking for a ready-made unit, and anyone who just bought (or was gifted) a telescope. No equipment is required for the first six lessons.
