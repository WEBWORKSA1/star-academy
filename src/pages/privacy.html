Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Star.Academy (“we”, “us”) handles information when you visit star.academy and related pages (the “Site”). We aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

1. Information you give us

When you submit a form (stargazing plan, newsletter/Sky Alerts, event booking, telescope help, trip planning, contest entry, donation pledge, job application, sponsorship inquiry or contact form), we receive the details you enter — typically your name, email address and message, and optionally phone number, city/country and preferences. Form submissions are delivered to us by email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form-processing service.

If you tick the optional partner-consent box, we may share your request with vetted partners (such as telescope retailers, planetariums, astronomy educators or tour operators) solely so they can respond to it. We never sell your personal information and never share it with partners without that consent.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Advertising cookies (Google AdSense). Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland), consent is requested through a certified consent-management message before personalized ads are shown.
  • Local storage. We store your preferences on your own device (chosen city or coordinates, night-vision mode, course progress, cookie choice, remembered email for form convenience). This data does not leave your browser unless you submit a form.
  • Browser-side data requests. To show live data, your browser connects directly to public services: NASA APOD API, NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center, The Space Devs (Launch Library 2), Where the ISS at?, Spaceflight News API, OpenStreetMap tiles, YouTube (privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com embeds that load only when you press play) and Google Fonts. These services may log your IP address under their own policies.
  • Location. If you click “Use my location”, your browser shares your approximate coordinates with our page to compute your sky. The coordinates are stored only on your device.

3. How we use information

To respond to requests, deliver your plan and alerts, run contests, process applications and sponsorships, improve the Site, prevent abuse, and comply with law. Our legal bases (where GDPR applies) are your consent, performance of a request you made, and our legitimate interests in operating a free educational site.

4. Retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose (generally up to 24 months), then delete them, unless law requires longer.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. Use our contact form to exercise these rights. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

6. Children

The Site is educational and suitable for families, but forms are intended for adults. Children under 16 (or the age required locally) should not submit personal information; the Young Astronomer contest category is entered by a parent or guardian.

7. Security & international transfers

The Site is served over HTTPS. Service providers may process data in other countries with appropriate safeguards.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the effective date above.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Please use our contact form.
