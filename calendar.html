About these predictions

Moon phases, eclipses, seasons and planetary events are calculated in your browser using the open-source astronomy-engine library, which models the Sun, Moon and planets to about an arcminute. Eclipse entries list the global event; whether you can see it depends on your location — lunar eclipses are visible from anywhere the Moon is above the horizon, while solar eclipses are only visible along specific paths.

Meteor-shower dates are the traditional peak nights. Rates are given as ZHR (the ideal hourly rate under perfect skies); expect fewer from towns and when the Moon is bright, which is why each entry includes a moonlight rating. For the best show, get away from lights and look between midnight and dawn. Read our meteor shower guide and eclipse safety guide.
