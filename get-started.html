Questions

Is the plan really free?

Yes. Star.Academy is funded by ads, sponsors and donations. If you opt in, we may introduce you to a partner (a retailer, planetarium or tour operator); you’re never obliged to buy anything.

Will you spam me?

No. You’ll receive your plan, occasional Sky Alerts for major events, and replies to anything you request. Every email has an unsubscribe option.

I already own a telescope — is this for me?

Absolutely. Tell us what you have and we’ll suggest targets, accessories that actually help, and observing programs to level up.
