Frequently asked questions

What can I see in the night sky tonight?

Open Tonight’s Sky, choose your city or share your location, and we calculate which planets are up, when it gets dark, the Moon’s phase and rise/set times, and draw a live sky chart — all in your browser.

Do I need a telescope to start stargazing?

No. Your eyes and a pair of 10×50 binoculars show the Moon’s craters, Jupiter’s moons, star clusters and even the Andromeda Galaxy. Our free course starts with naked-eye skills.

Is Star.Academy really free?

Yes. The site is supported by advertising, sponsors and voluntary donations so every lesson and tool stays free.

When is the next meteor shower or eclipse?

Our Sky Calendar computes the next 12 months of meteor showers, eclipses, full moons and planet events and lets you add each one to your calendar.

Is this site connected to the “Star Academy” TV show?

No. Star.Academy is an independent astronomy-education website and has no connection with any television format. See our trademark notice.
